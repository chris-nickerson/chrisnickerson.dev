How the inlet works

The browser generates the terrain and simulates the flight. You can grab the controls or leave the plane on autopilot.

In the air
Bank to turn. Wind changes as you fly through the valley.
On the water
Descend over the inlet and gently lift the nose before contact. The floats settle into the water. Power drops to idle on touchdown. Steer with A/D or the touch stick, add power to taxi, or choose Take off.
On the ground
The hillside is a 128 × 128 grid. Each patch of soil absorbs some water and passes the runoff downhill. Wet patches gradually turn green.
Over time
Your browser saves the soil moisture between visits.
